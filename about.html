About L&J Haven Stays

L&J Haven Stays is a small accommodation business founded by Thabile Jafta and Lerato Letlala. The business provides comfortable and welcoming accommodation for guests looking for peaceful stays in Salt Rock, KwaZulu-Natal.

L&J Haven Stays offers two accommodation options, Ocean Haven and Palm Retreat. Each property is designed to provide guests with a comfortable environment where they can relax, spend quality time with family and friends, and enjoy their stay.
